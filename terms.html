Users must provide accurate information and protect their account credentials. Sellers may publish only lawful products and must not mislead customers or infringe another person's rights. Listings may be removed for suspected fraud, abuse or policy violations.

Seller fees and commission

Seller registration and product listings are free. For each sale reported by a seller and independently verified by an Isoko Online Rwanda Admin as paid, the seller agrees to pay the marketplace a commission of 3% of the product sale amount. Delivery charges are excluded. The seller receives payment directly from the buyer in this release and is responsible for settling the commission with the marketplace under the agreed arrangement. The platform records commission due and records receipt only after the operator confirms it was received. A seller report alone is not proof of payment.

Orders and payments

This release uses WhatsApp to connect buyers and sellers. The platform does not collect, hold, or automatically settle buyer payments. Buyers and sellers confirm availability, delivery, payment, returns, and disputes directly. Mobile Money, bank, and Pi payment processing are not integrated in this release.

Any future payment feature must be separately configured, tested and verified on the Pi Platform before it can be described as available. Never share a wallet passphrase or private key.
