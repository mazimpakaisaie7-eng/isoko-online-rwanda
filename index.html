📣 Tumira abacuruzi kuri WhatsApp

Andika cyangwa ushyiremo nimero z’abacuruzi bemeye kwakira ubutumire. Shyiramo nimero imwe kuri buri murongo. Ubutumwa buzajya bufunguka muri WhatsApp yawe; kanda Send muri WhatsApp kugira ngo bwoherezwe.

Nta butumire burategurwa.

Icyitonderwa: WhatsApp ntitanga uburenganzira bwo kohereza ubutumwa buhishe cyangwa bwinshi mu buryo bwikora. Iyi buto ifungura ikiganiro n’ubutumwa bwuzuye; ni wowe ukanda Send. Irinde kohereza ubutumire ku bantu batabyemeye.
