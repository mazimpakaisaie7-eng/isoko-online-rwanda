Politiki y’ibanga

Ivugururwa: 25 Ugushyingo 2026

Amakuru dukusanya

Iyo ukoresheje konti, Supabase ishobora gutunganya aderesi ya email n’indangamuntu ya konti. Abacuruzi bashyiraho amakuru y’amaduka n’ibicuruzwa, harimo nimero ya WhatsApp n’amafoto.

Pi Network

Iyi verisiyo ishobora kugenzura izina n’indangamuntu ya Pi ukoresheje Pi Network Testnet. Access token ikoreshwa mu kugenzura konti kuri server kandi ntibikwa muri browser cyangwa muri database yacu. Iyi verisiyo ntabwo ikora ubwishyu bwa Pi.

Raporo z’ibicuruzwa byagurishijwe na commission

Abacuruzi bashobora kohereza raporo z’ibicuruzwa bagurishije, uburyo umukiriya yishyuye, reference y’ubwishyu n’ibisobanuro bifasha Admin kugenzura. Tubika raporo, commission ya 3%, uko yagenzuwe n’igihe yemejwe. Amakuru y’aho commission yageze ashobora kubikwa mu mateka y’ubucuruzi.

Amateka y’ibikorwa

Urubuga rubika ibikorwa by’ingenzi birimo gufungura cyangwa kuvugurura iduka, gushyiraho cyangwa guhindura igicuruzwa, no kohereza raporo y’igicuruzwa cyagurishijwe. Aya makuru afasha kurinda konti no kugenzura imikorere y’isoko.

Uko dukoresha amakuru

Amakuru akoreshwa mu kwerekana amaduka n’ibicuruzwa, gucunga konti z’abacuruzi, kugenzura raporo z’ubwishyu na commission no gufasha abakoresha. Amaduka n’ibicuruzwa byashyizwe ku isoko bishobora kugaragara ku mugaragaro.

Abatanga serivisi

Urubuga rukoresha Supabase mu kubika amakuru no kwinjiza abakoresha, na Pi Network mu kugenzura konti za Pi muri Testnet.

Umutekano

Ntuzigere wohereza ijambo ry’ibanga, Pi Wallet passphrase cyangwa private key ku muntu uwo ari we wese. Isoko Online Rwanda ntibisaba ayo makuru.

Ubufasha

Ongeraho aderesi ya email y’ubucuruzi ikurikiranwa mbere yo gutangaza urubuga ku mugaragaro.
