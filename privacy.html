Privacy Policy

Last updated: 24 November 2026

Information we process

When you create an account, Isoko uses the email address and account identifier provided by its authentication provider. Sellers may submit store and product details. Buyers may create order records containing selected products and payment references.

Payments

Pi authentication and payment processing are handled through Pi Network services. Isoko does not store your Pi Wallet passphrase or private keys. Payment status is recorded only after the server receives successful confirmation from the Pi Platform API.

How information is used

Information is used to display marketplace listings, manage seller listings, create orders, prevent payment fraud and provide support. Public store and product listings may be visible to visitors.

Third-party services

The marketplace uses Supabase for database and authentication services and Pi Network for Pi identity and payment features. Their own privacy terms also apply.

Contact

For privacy questions, use the contact details supplied by the marketplace operator. Do not send passwords, wallet passphrases or private keys.

Operator note: Before public launch, add a monitored business contact email and have this policy reviewed for the applicable laws and actual data practices.
